Understanding BP Stages

Know your numbers. The American Heart Association recognizes five blood pressure categories.

✓

Normal

SYS < 120
and
DIA < 80

Your blood pressure is in the ideal range. Keep up the healthy habits!

!

Elevated

SYS 120 - 129
and
DIA < 80

You're likely to develop high blood pressure unless steps are taken to control the condition.

!!

High BP (Stage 1)

SYS 130 - 139
or
DIA 80 - 89

Doctors are likely to prescribe lifestyle changes and may consider adding blood pressure medication.

!!!

High BP (Stage 2)

SYS 140+
or
DIA 90+

Doctors are likely to prescribe a combination of blood pressure medications and lifestyle changes.

⚠

Hypertensive Crisis

SYS 180+
and/or
DIA 120+

Consult your doctor immediately. If you have symptoms of organ damage, call 911.
