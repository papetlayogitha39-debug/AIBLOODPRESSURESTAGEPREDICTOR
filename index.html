What is Behavioral Biometrics?

Unlike physical biometrics (fingerprints, facial recognition), behavioral biometrics analyzes the unique patterns in human activity. It's not about what you are, but how you act.

⌨️

Keystroke Dynamics

Analyzes flight time (time between key presses) and dwell time (duration a key is held) to create a unique typing signature.

🖱️

Mouse Movement

Tracks cursor trajectory, speed, acceleration, and click patterns to differentiate legitimate users from bots or impostors.

📱

Touch Interactions

Measures swipe angles, pressure, and device orientation on mobile platforms for continuous verification.

The Science Behind the Scenes

Our platform leverages advanced machine learning models to establish a baseline of normal behavior for each user. As the user interacts with the application, we continuously score their current session against their established profile.

  • Privacy-First: We don't log what you type, only how you type.
  • Model Drift Mitigation: Profiles adapt dynamically over time to account for natural changes in behavior.
  • Low False Positives: High-dimensional feature extraction ensures extreme accuracy.
